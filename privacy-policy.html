1. Overview

Welcome to WordQuest: Teen Challenge ("we," "us," "our," or "Company"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our app. This Privacy Policy explains our information practices, what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

This Privacy Policy applies to our mobile application "WordQuest: Teen Challenge" available on Google Play Store and any related services we provide.

2. What Information We Collect

WordQuest: Teen Challenge is designed to collect minimal data. We do not collect personal information such as:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Location data or GPS information
  • Browsing history or device identifiers
  • Payment information (WordQuest: Teen Challenge is free)

What We Store Locally on Your Device:

  • Learning Progress: Your scores, XP points, completed challenges, and unlocked words
  • Game State: Your current streak, level progression, and game preferences
  • Session Data: How you play the game (challenge results, time spent)

All this data stays on your device only. We never access, collect, or transmit it to our servers.

3. How We Use Information

Since we collect no personal data and store everything locally on your device, there is no personal information to use or process. Your gameplay data serves only to:

  • Enable the app to function and track your learning progress
  • Provide a seamless experience when you reopen the app
  • Maintain your streak, XP, and unlocked words

4. How Information Is Stored

Local Storage Only

All gameplay data is stored exclusively on your mobile device using the device's local storage system. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not sent to our servers or any third-party services
  • Is encrypted by your device's operating system
  • Can only be accessed by the WordQuest: Teen Challenge app

Data Security

We take data security seriously. Your device's built-in security features (Android encryption, file permissions) protect your gameplay data.

Important: If you uninstall the app without backing up your data, all local progress will be permanently deleted. We recommend backing up your device regularly.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

WordQuest: Teen Challenge is designed for children ages 6 and up, including children under 13. We fully comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Why We Don't Collect Personal Data

To protect children's privacy, WordQuest: Teen Challenge:

  • Does not collect any personal information — no names, emails, birth dates, or identifiers
  • Does not require registration or login — just open and play
  • Does not share data — gameplay stays on the device
  • Does not use third-party tracking — no analytics, advertising networks, or analytics services collect data from the app
  • Does not contain advertising — no targeted ads, no data collection for advertising purposes

Parental Involvement

Since WordQuest: Teen Challenge stores all data locally and collects no personal information, there is no parental consent needed. However, parents can:

  • Monitor device storage to see the app's local data size
  • Back up device data regularly to preserve learning progress
  • Uninstall the app at any time

6. Information Sharing

We do not share any information with third parties.

Since we collect no personal data:

  • No data is sold, rented, or traded
  • No third-party advertisers receive any data
  • No analytics companies receive user information
  • No social media platforms receive data from the app

The only exception would be if required by law. If a government agency requests information, we will comply with legal requirements. However, since we collect no personal data, there would be nothing to provide.

7. Your Rights

Your Control Over Your Data

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

  • Access: You can view your data by playing the app — no special request needed
  • Delete: Uninstall the app to delete all game data from your device
  • Export: We provide no export feature because data never leaves your device anyway
  • Withdraw Consent: Not applicable — we never ask for personal information or consent

8. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date of this Privacy Policy.

Your continued use of the app after any changes means you accept the updated Privacy Policy.
